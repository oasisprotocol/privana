Introduction to Privana

Privana is a private, non-custodial DeFi interface. You can swap tokens, earn yield on stablecoins, and automate trading strategies without exposing your activity on-chain.

When you use a typical decentralized exchange, every trade you make is publicly visible. Bots watch for your transactions and can front-run them. Your entire wallet history is an open book. Privana was built to solve both of these problems.

Privana is a user-facing application built by the Oasis team. It runs on Oasis Sapphire, a confidential blockchain that executes smart contracts inside hardware-secured enclaves called Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs). Together, these give you privacy, automation, and self-custody, without the trade-offs that usually come with each.
