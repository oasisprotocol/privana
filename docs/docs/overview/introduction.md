Privana is a private, non-custodial DeFi interface. It lets you swap tokens, earn yield on stablecoins, and automate trading strategies — all without exposing your activity on-chain. This documentation explains how it works and the technology behind it.

When you use a typical decentralized exchange, every trade you make is publicly visible. Bots watch for your transactions and can front-run them. Your entire wallet history is an open book. Privana was built to solve both of these problems.

Privana is a **user-facing application** built by the [Oasis Network](https://oasis.net/) team. It runs on **Oasis Sapphire**, a confidential blockchain that executes smart contracts inside hardware-secured enclaves called Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs). Together, these give you privacy, automation, and self-custody — without the trade-offs that normally come with each.

These docs are split into four parts. **Getting Started** introduces the problem Privana solves and how the technology stack fits together. **Core Concepts** explains the vault model, TEEs, key encumbrance, and the custody model. **Features** walks through private swaps, yield, automation, AI agents, and the Telegram bot. **Underlying Technology** goes deeper into Oasis Sapphire, the system architecture, fallback mechanisms, and the academic research behind it all.
